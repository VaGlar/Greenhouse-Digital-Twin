## Caveat

The greenhouse in `papers/geothermiki-s192g-quote.md` uses 180-micron (0.18mm) film per layer, not the 1mm membranes this source describes — a real mismatch in film thickness. This was the best real double-layer-with-air-gap U-value figure found; it's a reasonable stand-in (thinner film with a properly inflated air gap should perform similarly, since the air gap — not the plastic itself — dominates the insulating effect) but not an exact match. Worth revisiting if a manufacturer datasheet for this specific film (Πλαστικών Κρήτης thermal/anti-drip PE) becomes available.
